Introduction to Statistics

Statistical Calculators

A reference tool for descriptive statistics, correlation, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and effect sizes. Results match base R output.

Descriptive Statistics

Compute measures of central tendency, dispersion, and shape for a single sample of numeric data.

Pearson Correlation

Test the linear association between two continuous variables. Reports r, its confidence interval via Fisher's z, and the test of H0: ρ = 0.

z-test

Tests whether a sample mean differs from a hypothesized population value when the population SD (σ) is known. Use the t-test tab if σ must be estimated from the sample.

Single-sample t-test

Tests whether a sample mean differs from a hypothesized value when the population SD is unknown and must be estimated from the sample.

Paired-sample t-test

Tests whether the mean difference between two matched measurements (e.g., before vs. after) differs from zero.

Independent-samples t-test

Compares means of two independent groups when the population SDs are unknown and assumed to be equal.

One-way Between-Groups ANOVA

Compares means across three or more independent groups. Reports F, p, and effect sizes (η², ω², partial η²).
